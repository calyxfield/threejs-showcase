What the sheet represents

The height is a softened, retarded scalar potential: each point responds to where each body was when its signal left. For each source we solve cτ = |x − r(t − τ)|, then add −1.7 / √(c²τ² + 0.42²) to the sheet height. This makes changes travel across the sheet with a finite delay. It is an illustration of a delayed field, not an Einstein-equation solver or a gravitational-wave waveform.

The bodies follow prescribed circular orbits, including before t = 0. The field does not drive their motion. The height and body size are exaggerated to make the shape visible. Colored rings mark signals emitted at regular intervals from the bodies’ past positions. They expand at the selected speed and are timing guides, not additional waves added to the height.

Changing signal speed restarts the illustration with the new speed. Drag or use arrow keys to turn the sheet; scroll or use +/− to zoom. Space pauses when the sheet has focus. Reset restarts time; Reset view restores the camera.
