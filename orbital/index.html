FLIGHT SYSTEM

Controls

Click two actual fuel tanks to pin their cards, then use In or Out. Flow is finite and requires tanks on the same ship or physically joined ships. Stop pauses transfer; closing cards leaves pumps running. Stop pumps before time warp.

Click an engine for its own group throttle, activation and bell switches. The small card leaves time running. Click an attached docking collar to undock immediately; dragging still orbits the camera.

Space fires the next applied stage once. A new Orbit ship starts with a dormant engine and an activation stage. Practice engines are already ready. Engine activation does not change throttle. Separation preserves manual throttle, cancels planned burns and leaves the released part in the world. Joined ships must undock before staging.

Stages opens the ordered stack. Drag numbered rows to reorder; drag action icons between rows to regroup them. Select a row or action for its editing options. Hover an icon to identify and highlight its hardware. Apply commits the draft; Cancel discards it. Flight edits affect pending stages only; fired IDs stay consumed. Blueprint edits never refit a live vessel.

In the map, click your orbit to place a maneuver. Edit prograde, radial-out and normal velocity, then Commit node. Negative values reverse each direction. Click another orbit point to move ignition. Preview transfer remains available for depot routes. The named Warp to next burn button advances to preparation and ignition; this is a guarded clock jump, not adjustable rate warp. Point manually at the gold bearing. Enter executes the due planned-duration burn; steering, translation or throttle cancels that assist.

G opens venue accounts. Ship trades and deposit/withdraw require stable four-foot support on a surface pad, or free flight inside an orbital station’s marked service zone below 0.10 m/s relative speed. Match velocity manually, stop rotation and cut thrust. Release any docking collar first; service never captures the ship. Deposits retain ownership without proceeds; stored stock can sell remotely into remaining bids. Airless Aster does not rotate. Goods move in whole 1,000 kg containers; fuel moves by kilogram. Books have finite depth.

Two-player-ship docking requires actual contact within 1 mm and less than 0.2 m/s, matching roll and axes. Choose a ship destination in the map; docking is enabled for the selected pair. Powered magnets can help a slow approach within 0.5 m; actual faces still must meet. After release, separate past 0.55 m and wait two seconds before returning. Depot capture uses its existing simplified adapter and the map's Capture depot button.
