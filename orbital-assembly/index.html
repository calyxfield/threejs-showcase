Assemble a blueprint

Choose a part. Move its ghost onto a visible connector and click to place. Green is clear; red explains why placement is blocked.

Q / E rotate around the attachment. C chooses another connector on the held part. Space resets rotation.

Click a placed part to lift it and its children. Esc cancels the move. Delete removes the held subtree; Ctrl Z restores it.

1 / 2 / 3 change view. Scroll to zoom; right-drag to orbit. The tinted boxes show conservative part clearance.

G opens all exchange books and your stored goods, from anywhere. Select an exchange to buy into its warehouse or sell stored goods. At a station, Warehouse transfers cargo and refuels from that local storage. Enter the marked zone, match velocity below 0.10 m/s and cut thrust. Deposits stay yours and pay no credits. Accounts and transfers pause time while open. Joined ships must undock before handling.

Flight reference: right-click an own core or docking port and choose Control from here. Right-click another craft’s port to Set as target. The navball uses the named control point: H/N forward/back, I/K down/up, J/L left/right, W/S pitch, A/D yaw and Q/E roll. Orbit/Target changes displayed velocity only; yellow markers show prograde/retrograde, pink the target, blue a valid maneuver and orange actual main thrust. Dashed markers are behind the control point. V changes Chase/Inertial follow without moving the view. Right-drag orbits; middle-drag pans; wheel zooms. F smoothly frames the whole craft. There is no automatic pointing or velocity cancellation. M switches to the orbit map. Drag to orbit Aster, scroll to zoom, and [ / ] select any actual ship or separated body. The map predicts unpowered coasting from current position and velocity; Click the selected orbit to place a maneuver. Edit its prograde, radial and normal components, then commit. Enter warps toward the earliest ship’s preparation point, 60 seconds before ignition. Point and use Z/X or Shift/Ctrl to burn manually. P pauses and remembers your manual rate. Comma slows time, period speeds it up and slash returns to 1×. No maneuver is needed. Thrust, steering, pumps and close approaches return to physical real time; free spin continues during passive warp. Ship corridors, surface approach and battery depletion stop accelerated coast. Restore always starts paused. W/S, A/D, Q/E steer; Shift/Ctrl adjust throttle; Z full, X cutoff; Space fires the next engine stage; T damping; CapsLock precision; [ / ] select a ship; B returns to its blueprint. The core contains wheels and a finite battery. Engines opens per-block enable switches. Axial separators release the entire attached subtree. Add their release icon to a stage; Spring push optionally supplies 0.2 m/s relative separation. Engines, fuel and core hardware stay with their parts. R toggles installed RCS; I/K, J/L and H/N translate; CapsLock uses 15% command precision. RCS blocks consume the selected body’s own tank propellant. Their placement determines force and torque. Compatible docking faces attract at short range and join at physical contact. Click a visible joined port to undock, then back clear before reapproach. Joined engines keep their own section’s fuel; the joined pair has one COM orbit and one maneuver node. Its selected section supplies full main-engine throttle in the preview; other main engines stay idle. Existing wheels/RCS act normally after the assumed initial alignment. Cut every section’s throttle before planning. Joined previews have a 60 s upper duration and a bounded work limit. A passive pair coasts to the earliest node with the other ships. Pumps, thrust and steering use real-time physics; battery depletion stops accelerated coast. There is no landing gear. Planet contact pauses the test. Ship pairs contact their conservative part boxes with bounded, inelastic frictional impulses; very fast motion or deep overlap pauses this prototype. Yard collisions are not simulated. Both separated bodies remain visible and selectable. Fuel opens finite-rate transfer between actual tanks on one ship or docked together. Choose From/To and kg/s, then Start, Stop or Reverse. Transfers stop when empty, full or disconnected; release retains each tank’s contents. Pumping near another ship or the surface stops conservatively.
